Pizza


Ingredients:

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp yeast
  • 3/4 cup warm water
  • 1/2 cup tomato sauce
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese

Steps:

  1. Preheat the oven to 475°F (245°C).
  2. Roll out the pizza dough on a floured surface.
  3. Transfer the dough to a pizza stone or baking sheet.
  4. Spread the tomato sauce evenly over the dough.
  5. Top with shredded mozzarella cheese and sliced pepperoni.
  6. Bake for 12-15 minutes, or until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is bubbly.
