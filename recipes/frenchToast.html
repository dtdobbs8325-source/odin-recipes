French Toast


Ingredients:

  • 4 slices of bread
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 2 tablespoons butter

Steps:

  1. Whisk together the eggs, milk, vanilla extract, cinnamon, and salt in a shallow dish.
  2. Dip each slice of bread into the egg mixture, allowing it to absorb the liquid.
  3. Heat the butter in a skillet over medium heat.
  4. Place the bread slices in the skillet and cook until golden brown on both sides.
